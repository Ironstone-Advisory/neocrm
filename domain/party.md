# Party

A **Party** is an entity capable of participating in a relationship, activity, opportunity, deal, contract, commitment, or action.

## Types

- **Person** — an individual.
- **Company** — a legal or operating organization.
- **Household** — a group of people with a shared relationship context.

Party type is not business role. A Person, Company, or Household may independently be a Customer, Prospect, Partner, Supplier, Competitor, Employee, Decision Maker, or Contract Party.

## Company structure

Companies may be related to divisions, business units, subsidiaries, partner companies, and people. These are modelled as relationships with explicit semantics. A division does not automatically become a new Party type.
