# ADR-0005: Governed action and provenance

**Status:** Accepted for v0.1

## Decision

NeoCRM must retain provenance and epistemic state for material claims. Consequential external actions are proposed and previewed before execution, then recorded in an auditable form.

## Consequences

- The system cannot present an inference as a source fact.
- Human approval is a first-class part of the action path.
- Adapter contracts must expose retrieval freshness, authority, and execution results.
