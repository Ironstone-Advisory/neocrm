# LinkedIn launch draft

What if we designed CRM from scratch with AI agents as the foundation—rather than adding AI to database schemas, forms and reports?

That question led me to NeoCRM: a falsifiable experiment in AI-first, agent-native relationship management.

The architecture separates seven responsibilities, from the user experience and specialist agents through relationship orchestration, context, storage, integration and infrastructure. Shared semantics and governance cross every layer. Zoho and Obsidian remain durable sources; they do not become the product's mental model.

We now have a deterministic, read-only reference slice running on synthetic fixtures. Its specification, tests and safety evaluation pass locally. That is engineering evidence—not proof of live usefulness, time savings or business results.

The first real experiment will compare:

- A: the normal Zoho workflow;
- B: NeoCRM with Zoho; and
- C: NeoCRM with Zoho plus permissioned Obsidian context.

I have written up the design, the evidence we have, what it does not prove, and the conditions under which we should stop or change direction.
