# Functional Requirements

## Context assembly

- The system must resolve the relevant Party, relationship, commercial matter, and time period before gathering context.
- It must assemble a relationship brief from appropriate operational, knowledge, activity, calendar, and external sources.
- It must preserve source-level provenance for material claims.

## Relationship intelligence

- It must support people, companies, and households as Parties.
- It must represent business roles independently of Party type.
- It must support multi-party relationships, company structure, commercial relationships, and household membership.
- It must surface activity, commitments, inactivity, and temporal changes across a relationship.

## Time and load

- It must represent events, intervals, commitments, availability, capacity, workload, and time series.
- It must render a calendar as a view of the temporal model.
- It must support questions about meeting load, outstanding commitments, relationship attention, and allocation of time.

## Governed action

- It must propose actions with rationale, target system, and expected effect before execution.
- It must require explicit user approval for consequential external actions.
- It must record the result and provenance of executed actions.
