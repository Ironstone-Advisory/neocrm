|---|---|---|---|

## Competitive whitespace

No reviewed product clearly combines all of the following as its primary product contract:

1. portable Party → Role → Relationship semantics independent of the transactional store;
2. claim-level provenance and epistemic state across people, agents, CRM, documents, and conversations;
3. relationship-aware recommendations spanning sales, service, engagement, commitments, and time;
4. policy-as-data with purpose, consent, jurisdiction, evidence quality, authority, and action-risk evaluation;
5. one human-readable chain from observation through inference, approval, external write, verification, result, and correction;
6. a conversational assistant that can propose both work and semantic-model changes without silently mutating either;
7. portable human-owned knowledge in Obsidian while a replaceable CRM such as Zoho remains operational.

That whitespace is narrower and more defensible than “AI CRM” by itself.

## Adoption implications

- **SMB buyers** value quick setup, simple pricing, dependable follow-up, meeting capture, and visible value. Apollo, Pipedrive, HubSpot, and Zoho establish the usability bar.
- **Mid-market buyers** add forecasting, governance, data integration, account planning, coaching, service, and change control. Salesforce, Microsoft, Creatio, Gong, Outreach, and Salesloft/Clari Forecast establish the control bar.
- **Marketing-led teams** expect real-time triggers, adaptive journeys, experiments, consent, and cross-channel orchestration. Marketo, Braze, 6sense, and Demandbase establish the decisioning bar.
- **NeoCRM should prove one cross-system relationship loop first**, then add reusable semantic extensions. It should not reproduce every execution channel, data marketplace, ad network, help desk, billing engine, or commerce platform.
