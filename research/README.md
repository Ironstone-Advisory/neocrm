# Research

The cleaned product-origin record is [`../spec/vision/origin.md`](../spec/vision/origin.md). It preserves the originating agent-native idea while separating adopted principles from historical market and productivity claims.

This directory is for source material that informs the thesis, model, and experiments. Preserve original artifacts when their license and confidentiality status permit it. For each source, record:

- title, author, date, and stable location;
- why it matters to NeoCRM;
- claims adopted, challenged, or left open;
- quotation or extraction boundaries;
- licensing and confidentiality notes.

Research should inform decisions and experiments; it should not be treated as a substitute for testing the NeoCRM model.

Maintain a claim ledger for any article or product assertion: claim, source/evidence, date, scope, limitations, and permitted wording. Architecture hypotheses and synthetic CAP-001 results must not be presented as proven productivity, customer, revenue, market, or production-capability claims.
