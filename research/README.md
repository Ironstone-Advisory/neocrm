# Research

This directory is for source material that informs the thesis, model, and experiments. Preserve original artifacts when their license and confidentiality status permit it. For each source, record:

- title, author, date, and stable location;
- why it matters to NeoCRM;
- claims adopted, challenged, or left open;
- quotation or extraction boundaries;
- licensing and confidentiality notes.

Research should inform decisions and experiments; it should not be treated as a substitute for testing the NeoCRM model.
