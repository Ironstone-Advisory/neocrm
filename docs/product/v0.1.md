# Historical product scaffold

The repository's original product document framed v0.1 as a transactional CRM foundation. The recovered design context establishes a different v0.1 boundary:

> NeoCRM is a semantic architecture and experiment in AI-first relationship intelligence—not a finished CRM application.

The authoritative product framing is in [the thesis](../vision/thesis.md), [principles](../vision/principles.md), [semantic requirements](../requirements/semantic-model.md), and [experiments](../experiments/README.md).
