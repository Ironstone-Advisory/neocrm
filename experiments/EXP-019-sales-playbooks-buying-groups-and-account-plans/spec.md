# EXP-019: Sales playbooks, buying groups, and account plans

**Status:** Planned

## Decision and hypothesis

Relationship-scoped plans, buying-group projections, explicit sequence lifecycles, and mutual Commitments can improve coordinated sales work without inventing duplicate customer ontologies.

## Participants and boundaries

Buying groups remain Party/Role/Relationship projections. Account plans are Plans; externally visible mutual plans are Commitments. Sequence delivery is initially proposed/draft-only; later delivery requires consent and a WriteGrant.

## Design

Test plan creation, stakeholder gaps, enrollment, branching, cadence, stop rules, delivery attempts, handoffs, digital-room projection, customer correction, and service/consent conflicts.

## Measures and decision rule

Measure plan usefulness, stakeholder/evidence accuracy, stopped-contact correctness, coordination effort, commitment clarity, customer effort, time/cost, and harm. Any stop-rule override or role inference presented as fact fails.

## Results record

Record plan/sequence versions, projection evidence, decisions, receipts, outcomes, customer/human feedback, and limitations.
