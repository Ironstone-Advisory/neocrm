# EXP-001 human evaluation

Score each item from 1 (not useful/unsafe) to 5 (excellent), with comments and material errors recorded:

1. Correct subject and relationship context.
2. Material fact accuracy and citation trust.
3. Material context completeness within the declared boundary.
4. Clear separation of evidence, interpretation, uncertainty, conflict, and Recommendation.
5. Relevance of Goal, conversation Plan/questions, and next step.
6. Reduced preparation effort and source switching.
7. Respect for consent, privacy, customer interests, and human judgment.
8. Overall usefulness.

Capture preparation time, source switches, corrections, missing material context, unsafe/irrelevant advice, and condition preference. Prefer an independent evaluator and blinded B/C comparison where feasible.

Target: median C usefulness and overall usefulness at least 4/5; median paired C improvement at least one point over B; C preferred in at least 70% of comparable cases; at least 20 percentage-point material-context recall improvement over B; at least 30% lower preparation time than A; no material accuracy decline; zero hard-stop failures.
