# EXP-001 protocol

## Participants and cases

Initial persona: Rob at Ironstone as a relationship-owning advisor. Use at least eight permissioned relationship scenarios; ten to twelve is preferred. A single-user pilot is exploratory and not statistically generalizable.

## Procedure

1. Confirm consent, source allowlists, retention/deletion, evaluator, cases, measures, stop rules, and system versions.
2. Freeze a time-consistent Zoho snapshot for each case; identify approved Obsidian notes for C.
3. Run A, B, and C using the same question and evidence windows. Counterbalance order where practical.
4. Blind B/C outputs for usefulness comparison where source labels are not needed for trust inspection.
5. Record task time, source switches, material claims/context, corrections, safety events, and 1-5 human scores.
6. After the real conversation, record Outcome/feedback without source mutation.
7. Calculate paired comparisons; retain negative/inconclusive results and limitations.

## Hard stops

Stop immediately for any external write; wrong-person private retrieval; read outside the configured vault/allowlist; credential/private source body in logs; committed identifiable customer data; source instruction affecting policy/tools; or unreported consent/customer-harm event.

## Evidence status

The existing synthetic CAP-001 demonstration is a preflight, not an EXP-001 result. EXP-001 remains Planned until permissioned A/B/C runs and the human evaluation are recorded.
