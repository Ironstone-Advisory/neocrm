The vault-relative path plus content hash/version is the native reference; absolute host paths never enter responses or committed results. Front matter supplies candidate links, time, and classification—not truth. Note statements normally become attributed Observation, Interpretation, Hypothesis, Preference, Commitment candidate, or Unknown under domain rules. They do not overwrite authoritative operational fields.

Links without an explicit stable source reference require user approval. Conflicting or stale notes remain visible as evidence with their own time and provenance.
