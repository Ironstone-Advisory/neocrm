# EXP-001: Zoho + Obsidian relationship brief

**Status:** Planned

**Authority boundary:** EXP-001 is read-only. Later experimental WriteGrants do not broaden it.

## Question

Can a governed context/insight Agent help a relationship owner prepare for and improve a consequential conversation by combining authoritative Zoho operational state with permissioned Obsidian relationship knowledge, while reducing effort and preserving provenance, uncertainty, consent, and human judgment?

## Hypotheses

- B versus A tests the value of NeoCRM's conversational/context synthesis over the same Zoho evidence.
- C versus B tests the incremental value of approved Obsidian context.
- C must not reduce material accuracy or violate any hard-stop control.

## Conditions

- **A — Zoho baseline:** prepare using the normal relevant Zoho record experience.
- **B — NeoCRM + Zoho:** receive a governed relationship brief from the same Zoho snapshot.
- **C — NeoCRM + Zoho + Obsidian:** receive the same NeoCRM brief with explicit approved Obsidian notes added.

## Required output

Resolved subject/identity confidence; why the relationship matters now; authoritative operational facts; contextual observations/interpretations; current commercial/service state; Activities/Conversations and Commitments; conflicts, stale/unavailable sources, and Unknowns; relevant Goal; recommended conversation Plan/questions; and expandable provenance. No external write or contact is permitted.

After the conversation, capture structured user feedback, intended/observed Outcome, and LearningSignal under the experiment protocol. This capture cannot change source records, policy, Agent behavior, or durable memory automatically.

## Claim boundary

EXP-001 may test context assembly, one bounded Agent role, human-Agent usefulness, administrative effort, provenance/epistemic discipline, and a small recommendation/outcome-feedback loop. It cannot establish a production agent workforce, autonomous selling/service, full sales-service-marketing unification, production security, revenue impact, or general superiority to established CRM.

The committed fixture implementation is a technical preflight only. It does not advance this experiment beyond Planned. See the [operator runbook](runbook.md) and [fixture evidence record](fixture-results.md), as well as the [protocol](protocol.md), [source boundary](source-boundary.md), [human evaluation](human-evaluation.md), and [preregistration](preregistration.md).
