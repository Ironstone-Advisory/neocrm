# EXP-003: Time and load intelligence

## Hypothesis

A temporal model that combines events, commitments, relationship activity, availability, and workload produces better attention-allocation recommendations than a calendar alone.

## Question

Given active opportunities, relationship activity, outstanding commitments, and a constrained week, can the system propose a defensible allocation of follow-up time?

## Success criteria

- Uses relationship importance and evidence of momentum, not only due dates.
- Separates scheduled events from commitments and inferred workload.
- Explains constraints and trade-offs.
- Leaves final prioritization with the user.
