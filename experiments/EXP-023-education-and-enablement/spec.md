# EXP-023: Education, coaching, and enablement

**Status:** Planned

## Decision and hypothesis

Transparent skill/competency paths and bounded simulations can improve learning and relationship work while avoiding opaque employee surveillance.

## Participants and boundaries

Participants know what data is used, for what purpose, who sees results, retention, correction, and appeal. Initial cases use synthetic simulations. Coaching evidence cannot authorize employment decisions or silently become a personnel profile.

## Design

Compare self-directed baseline, generic coaching, and NeoCRM evidence-linked simulation/feedback. Test rubrics, learner goals, accessibility, correction/appeal, certification expiry, evaluator bias, and data minimization.

## Measures and decision rule

Measure skill improvement, feedback usefulness, assessment reliability/fairness, learner effort/trust, correction/appeal, privacy, and cost. Hidden monitoring, unexplained scoring, or secondary use is a hard stop.

## Results record

Record versions, consent/notice, rubric evidence, learner feedback, appeals/corrections, aggregate outcomes, and limitations; do not commit identifiable employee content.

## Proposed addition (2026-10-25): benchmark-practitioner exemplars

One tempting design is to mine a top seller's calls, email and proposals and coach everyone else toward that pattern. It is in scope only as **exemplars, not a scoring model**: the benchmark person gives informed consent, is credited, can withdraw, and sees what is extracted; others are shown examples, not ranked against a person. Relationship-skill proxies (talk ratio, validation before pivoting, stakeholder coverage) are observations about a conversation, never inferences about anyone's emotional intelligence or character. Any drift from exemplar to ranking is the hidden-scoring hard stop above.
