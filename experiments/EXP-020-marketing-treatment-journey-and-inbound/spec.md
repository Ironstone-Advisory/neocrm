# EXP-020: Marketing treatments, adaptive journeys, intent, and inbound handoff

**Status:** Planned

## Decision and hypothesis

Explicit assignment/exposure/attribution and governed journey recommendations can improve relevant engagement while reducing unwanted contact and false person-level intent claims.

## Participants and boundaries

Initial adaptive-journey and intent activation output is recommendation-only. Inbound qualification may propose booking/routing and typed handoff. Cross-channel delivery requires separate grants; consent, suppression, negative response, service state, frequency, and harm guardrails always win.

## Design

Preregister treatments, variants, cohorts, assignments, holdouts, exposures, outcome windows, guardrails, stop rules, and attribution methods. Test decaying account intent, ambiguous person attribution, inbound identity, routing, booking, and contamination/noncompliance.

## Measures and decision rule

Measure incremental customer/business outcomes, exposure integrity, attribution uncertainty, false activation, consent/contact safety, handoff quality, effort, and cost. Unregistered assignment changes, false causal claims, or mandatory-stop violations fail.

## Results record

Preserve preregistration, assignment/exposure evidence, analyses, alternative explanations, adverse outcomes, and de-identified receipts.
