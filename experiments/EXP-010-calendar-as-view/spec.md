# EXP-010: Calendar as a view over Time

**Status:** Planned

Test whether adding or replacing a calendar adapter preserves Event, Commitment, Availability, Capacity, Workload, deadline, and Goal-horizon meaning across calendar, timeline, and relationship views.

Measure round-trip fidelity, mapping loss, occurrence versus observation time, recurrence/time-zone behavior, availability privacy, conflict disclosure, and CTS-11/13/16. Calendar-provider vocabulary must not redefine Time semantics.
