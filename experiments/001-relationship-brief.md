# EXP-001: Unified relationship brief

## Hypothesis

A relationship brief assembled from operational data, activity, calendar, and knowledge context improves preparation for a real conversation compared with a CRM-only brief.

## Question

Can the system answer: *What do I need to know before I speak with this person?* without the user naming the underlying systems?

## Setup

Use a representative relationship with an associated company, current commercial matter, recent interactions, commitments, and relevant notes.

## Success criteria

- Correctly resolves the Party and commercial context.
- Retrieves material facts from appropriate sources.
- Distinguishes evidence from inference.
- Identifies commitments, risks, and a sensible next action.
- Shows source gaps and uncertainty rather than hallucinating a complete answer.

## Implications

Success supports the Relationship Intelligence Layer. Failure should identify whether the problem is entity resolution, semantic mapping, retrieval, provenance, or reasoning.
