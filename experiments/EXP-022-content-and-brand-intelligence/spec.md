# EXP-022: Content and brand intelligence

**Status:** Planned

## Decision and hypothesis

Versioned assets, messages, brand policies, rights, approvals, variants, and evidence can improve content relevance and compliance without losing authorship or source truth.

## Participants and boundaries

Use approved content and rights. Generation is proposed until accepted; high-risk claims require human approval. Delivery is out of scope unless separately granted. Customer data is minimized and not repurposed for training.

## Design

Test retrieval, key-message selection, variant generation, brand/legal/accessibility checks, rights/expiry, source citation, human editing, and performance evidence across routine and adversarial cases.

## Measures and decision rule

Measure policy/claim/rights accuracy, source validity, usefulness, edit distance, accessibility, time/cost, and adverse outcomes. Fabricated evidence, expired rights use, or unapproved high-risk claims fail.

## Results record

Retain content/policy/generator versions, reviewer disposition, differences, evidence, outcomes, and de-identified receipts.
