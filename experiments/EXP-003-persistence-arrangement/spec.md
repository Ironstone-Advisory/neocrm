# EXP-003: Same questions and data, different persistence

**Status:** Planned

Represent identical versioned canonical fixtures in two physical arrangements while holding questions, Agent/orchestration versions, policy, and expected evidence constant.

Compare semantic answer equivalence, provenance equivalence, ordering/temporal behavior, latency/cost, and unsupported-semantics logs. A difference caused only by physical storage falsifies the relevant persistence-independence claim unless it is a declared capability difference.
