# EXP-025: Packaging, transparent metering, and outcome pricing

**Status:** Planned

## Decision and hypothesis

A core relationship control plane plus separable outcome modules and understandable variable-work metering will be clearer and fairer than opaque credits or safety-tiering.

## Participants and boundaries

Research uses consented buyer/customer interviews, prototypes, and synthetic invoices before any billing change. Core provenance, consent, policy, readable history, correction, portability, Levels 0-2, and basic usage visibility are never removed. No participant is charged during concept testing.

## Design

Compare base-org, seat, module, variable-work, and carefully defined outcome-pricing concepts. Show pre-action estimates and post-action reconciliations for success, partial, failure, cancellation, compensation, and reversal. Test disputes, budget stops, attribution ambiguity, gaming, and adverse incentives.

## Measures and decision rule

Measure comprehension, predictability, willingness/value evidence, dispute resolution, attribution confidence, incentive safety, customer outcomes, and operating economics. Hidden units, billing failed work as success, non-disputable claims, or harmful incentives fail.

## Results record

Version packaging/pricing stimuli, assumptions, segments, responses, calculations, disputes, negative findings, limitations, and resulting product decision.
