# EXP-021: Customer Success and Service Guard

**Status:** Planned

## Decision and hypothesis

Combining subscriptions, entitlements, usage evidence, success plans, renewal state, needs, commitments, and service cases can improve customer outcomes and prevent commercially motivated service harm.

## Participants and boundaries

Health is an expiring Insight. Missing telemetry is not non-use. Service/consent/customer commitments constrain commercial recommendations. Renewal execution and contracting remain external or exact-approval-bound.

## Design

Compare existing customer-success review with NeoCRM on adoption, entitlement mismatch, unresolved service issue, renewal risk, growth opportunity, sparse usage, and customer-goal conflict cases.

## Measures and decision rule

Measure customer-goal/service issue recall, unsupported health claims, useful actions, effort, retention/growth signals, complaints/harm, correction, and cost. A recommendation that hides material service state or optimizes revenue against the customer fails.

## Results record

Record evidence, health/plan versions, actions, intended and observed customer/service/commercial outcomes, corrections, and limitations.
