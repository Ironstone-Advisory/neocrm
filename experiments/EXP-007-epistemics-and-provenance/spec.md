# EXP-007: Epistemic categories and provenance

**Status:** Planned

Test whether explicit Fact, Observation, Interpretation, Hypothesis, Unknown, and Conflict treatment plus inspectable provenance improves accuracy, calibration, correction, and trust while preventing unsafe write-back. Recommendation is evaluated separately as a decision artifact.

Compare labeling/citation accuracy, unsupported-claim rate, conflict retention, Unknown calibration, source inspection, correction quality, and unsafe-action prevention. CAP-001 tests exercise contract mechanics but do not replace representative human evaluation.
