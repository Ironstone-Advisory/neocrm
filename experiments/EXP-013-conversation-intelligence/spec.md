# EXP-013: Evidence-linked conversation intelligence

**Status:** Planned

## Decision and hypothesis

For approved meetings/messages, evidence-linked TranscriptSegments, SpeakerAttributions, ExtractionProposals, and CommitmentCandidates will reduce capture effort without increasing unsupported canonical assertions versus manual review.

## Participants and boundaries

Use consented or synthetic content, purpose-limited access, declared retention, and a human reviewer. Extraction has no write authority beyond proposed objects. A candidate becomes canonical only through the registered promotion policy.

## Design

Compare manual capture, unstructured model summary, and NeoCRM evidence-linked review on the same cases. Blind reviewers where practical; include ambiguous speakers, negation, corrections, conflicting commitments, and prompt injection. Preserve exact source spans and model/prompt/schema versions.

## Measures and decision rule

Measure claim/commitment precision and recall, citation validity, speaker accuracy, correction time, reviewer trust, privacy/safety events, and cost. Any silent promotion, fabricated evidence, or private-source boundary violation is a hard stop.

## Results record

Record accepted/corrected/rejected proposals, errors, missingness, timing, cost, safety events, and human evaluation. Planned specification is not evidence.
