# EXP-012: Temporal relationship analysis

**Status:** Planned

Test whether time-series and sequence analysis detects meaningful momentum, latency, service risk, commitment drift, and relationship change without treating correlation as Fact.

Measure signal precision/recall, lead time, stability, explanation, false alarms, missing-data sensitivity, subgroup/customer-harm effects, cost, and later Outcome association. Promotion from Signal to Recommendation or Action requires separate interpretation and policy.
