# EXP-024: Bounded supervisor agent and active management

**Status:** Planned

## Decision and hypothesis

A bounded supervisor Agent can improve prioritization, escalation, and workload visibility at autonomy Level 2 without opaque surveillance or self-expanding authority.

## Participants and boundaries

Start with recommendation/escalation only. Expose evidence, rationale, uncertainty, cost/value projection, fairness/workload constraints, and appeal. Territory, quota, performance management, and irreversible assignment are out of scope. A later reversible Level 3 assignment condition requires ADR-0016 authority.

## Design

Compare human prioritization with NeoCRM recommendations on workload, stalled commitments, service risk, opportunity, budget, absence, and conflicting goals. Test override, appeal, biased/incomplete data, grant expiry, and safe escalation.

## Measures and decision rule

Measure useful prioritization, missed/false escalation, workload fairness, override/appeal, transparency, task outcomes, human effort, agent cost, and harm. Covert monitoring, self-granting, or unauthorized assignment fails.

## Results record

Record recommendation/action versions, inputs and gaps, decisions, overrides, workload and outcome measures, incidents, cost/value assumptions, and participant feedback.
