# ADR-0019: Keep integrity core and test outcome modules with transparent metering

**Decision status:** Accepted

**Implementation status:** Planned

**Evidence maturity:** Planned

**Validation plan:** EXP-025 / EVAL-004

## Context

AI CRM vendors mix seats, editions, add-ons, credits, and usage. Opaque credits obscure cost and can make safety controls appear optional. Outcome pricing can also create perverse incentives when outcomes are vague or weakly attributable.

## Decision

The Relationship Control Plane is core in every plan: semantic portability, provenance, consent, policy, readable history, correction, bounded autonomy Levels 0-2, and basic usage/spend visibility. Advanced value hypotheses may be packaged as modules only after evidence.

EXP-025 may test base-organization, human-seat, outcome-module, and genuine variable-work charges. Metering must be human-readable, estimated before action, reconciled after action, budgeted, and linked to completion, failure, cancellation, reversal, compensation, and Outcome.

Outcome pricing is allowed only for predefined, inspectable, disputable, safely incentivized, attributable outcomes with limitations visible to the customer. It must not reward unwanted contact, manipulation, hidden surveillance, service degradation, or revenue at the customer's expense.

## Consequences

- Safety and audit integrity cannot be removed from lower-priced plans.
- Commercial hypotheses remain separate from evidence that buyers value them.
- Commercial Operations remains optional, later, and adapter-led.
- Pricing and packaging changes require experiment evidence and a versioned product decision.
