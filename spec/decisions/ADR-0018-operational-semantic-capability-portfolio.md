## Context

Competitive research found broad feature gaps, but most are operational lifecycle and experience gaps rather than failures of the Party/Role/Relationship, evidence, policy, action, time, and outcome foundation.

## Decision

Adopt the capability portfolio and canonical objects in Decisions D16-D35. Reuse existing semantics before adding objects: buying groups are projections; qualification scores are Insights; account plans are Plans; mutual action plans use Commitments; agent cost/value begins as a projection. Add first-class objects only for independently governed lifecycles such as archive disposition, reconciliation, transcript extraction, sequences, forecasts, treatment assignments, entitlements, content governance, and competency evidence.

All new objects expose identity, temporal validity, provenance, state, responsible Actor, policy, correction, and outcome links appropriate to their lifecycle. Product views are projections, never competing ontologies.

## Consequences

- The portfolio remains cross-functional rather than separate sales, marketing, and service databases.
- Capability specifications are Accepted; implementation and evidence remain Planned.
- EXP-013 through EXP-024 determine which capabilities earn further investment.
- Territory/quota/work balancing remains deferred pending a separately accepted experiment.
