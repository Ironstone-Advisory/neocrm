# Principles

1. **Agents and intelligence are foundational.** Relationship goals, collaboration, context, policy, and outcomes organize the product.
2. **People remain accountable.** Agents operate under owned purpose, explicit delegation, bounded autonomy, and escalation.
3. **One relationship, several perspectives.** Sales, service, and marketing use shared semantics rather than separate customer models.
4. **Proactivity is policy-bound.** Human requests, events, schedules, and signals may initiate sensing and planning; they do not create permission for opaque customer effects.
5. **NeoCRM owns meaning.** Adapters and stores implement canonical semantics; they do not define them.
6. **Persistence is replaceable.** Operational, analytical, graph, semantic, memory, event, and audit roles are required; particular products are not.
7. **Types, roles, and relationships are different.** Types describe what an entity is; roles describe contextual business meaning; relationships describe connections.
8. **Activity is canonical; channels are implementations.** Message and Conversation retain distinct meaning across email, SMS, calls, meetings, and other channels.
9. **Time is a domain.** Calendar is a view over Events, Commitments, Availability, Capacity, Workload, and temporal relationships.
10. **Context is minimum necessary.** Resolve identity first; retrieve only authorized evidence required for the Goal; do not disclose protected record existence on denial.
11. **Provenance is first class.** Source authority, freshness, confidence, completeness, and epistemic category remain independent and visible.
12. **Recommendation is not fact.** Fact, Observation, Interpretation, Hypothesis, Unknown, and Conflict are epistemic/response categories; Recommendation is a decision artifact.
13. **Models do not hold credentials or execute effects.** Policy and isolated action gateways mediate tools, approval, verification, compensation, and audit.
14. **Learning is governed.** Outcomes produce LearningSignals and change proposals, never silent self-modification.
15. **Views are projections.** Conversation, graph, timeline, calendar, commercial, service, campaign, evidence, and outcome views do not become competing ontologies.
16. **Customer value constrains growth.** Revenue goals are balanced by consent, service quality, fairness, trust, avoidance of manipulation and harm, and redress.
17. **Claims require scoped evidence.** Synthetic conformance, human usefulness, operational results, customer outcomes, and causal business evidence are distinct.
