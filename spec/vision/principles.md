# Principles

1. **AI-first, not database-first.** Intelligence and context assembly organize the experience.
2. **Semantic ownership.** NeoCRM defines what domain concepts mean; adapters define where data lives.
3. **Replaceable persistence.** No CRM, note-taking system, mail provider, or calendar is the architecture.
4. **Types, roles, and relationships are different.** Types describe what an entity is; roles describe its business meaning; relationships describe how entities connect.
5. **Activity is canonical; channels are implementations.** Email, SMS, WhatsApp, calls, and meetings are sources of activities.
6. **Time is a domain.** Calendar is a view over events, commitments, availability, capacity, workload, and time series.
7. **Provenance is first class.** A useful answer identifies source, time, confidence, and reasoning boundary.
8. **Reasoning is bounded.** Facts, observations, interpretations, hypotheses, and recommendations must remain distinguishable.
9. **Human authority is preserved.** Consequential actions require review and explicit approval.
10. **Experiments decide.** Architecture claims remain hypotheses until tested with representative relationship work.
