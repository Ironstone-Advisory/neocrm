# Goals and non-goals

**Status:** Accepted

## P0 goals

- Define one coherent agent-native architecture with explicit layer and control boundaries.
- Make Agent, Goal, Plan, Run, Delegation, Context, PolicyDecision, Approval, Action, Outcome, LearningSignal, Consent, and Preference first-class.
- Preserve one canonical relationship model across sales, service, and engagement perspectives.
- Demonstrate a safe read-only relationship brief with identity-first, minimum-necessary retrieval, provenance, uncertainty, and no external writes.
- Run EXP-001 as a controlled comparison of Zoho baseline, NeoCRM over Zoho, and NeoCRM over Zoho plus permissioned Obsidian context.
- Preserve CAP-001 and EXP-001 as read-only evidence while specifying later authority separately.

## P1 goals

- Exercise typed human-Agent and Agent-Agent collaboration around Goals and Plans.
- Add relationship, agent, work, approval, insight, graph, timeline, time/capacity, commercial, service, campaign, evidence, outcome, and administration views.
- Add read-only source ports, storage ports, event contracts, and safe model gateway contracts.
- Capture post-work Outcomes and LearningSignals without online self-modification.
- Test the approved operational portfolio through EXP-013 through EXP-024 and transparent product-value hypotheses through EXP-025.
- Support registered multi-workflow automation under explicit time-bounded grants, with fresh exact human authorization for every deletion.

## Non-goals for v0.1

- Replacing every CRM, inbox, calendar, knowledge base, ERP, or service platform.
- Claiming demonstrated live Zoho/Obsidian capability before permissioned opt-in runs and evidence exist.
- Unbounded autonomous customer contact, pricing, contracting, or unregistered multi-step execution.
- A universal customer master, unrestricted data replication, or one mandatory database product.
- Silent learning from customer data, self-granted authority, or model access to credentials/action handles.
- Proving revenue lift, market size, or industry disruption from synthetic fixtures or a single-user pilot.
- Treating product approval, a generated schema, or a Planned experiment as implementation or evidence.

## Product constraint

A scope addition that weakens identity, consent, minimum context, provenance, independent policy, human accountability, or evidence honesty is not an acceleration; it is outside the NeoCRM product contract.
