# Business capabilities

**Status:** Proposed canonical capability map

## BC-01 Understand the relationship

Resolve Parties and identities; assemble the relationship graph and timeline; understand roles, needs, preferences, consent, history, commitments, commercial/service state, evidence gaps, conflicts, and relevant change.

## BC-02 Manage shared relationship goals

Define customer, relationship, service, commercial, engagement, and ethical Goals; identify beneficiaries and trade-offs; link them to Parties, Relationships, Journeys, Cases, Opportunities, Campaigns, Contracts, Plans, and Outcomes.

## BC-03 Collaborate through specialist agents

Define, delegate, plan, coordinate, hand off, monitor, and evaluate bounded work among people and specialist relationship, research, sales, service, engagement, policy, and evaluation Agents.

## BC-04 Sense, recommend, and act proactively

Respond to human requests, events, schedules, Signals, and outcome gaps; identify opportunity, risk, unmet commitments, service concerns, and journey discontinuity; recommend, draft, or execute only within policy and delegation.

## BC-05 Unify sales, service, and engagement

Present these as coordinated perspectives over shared relationship semantics, ensuring one function cannot optimize without relevant consent, commitments, service state, customer need, and other functions' work.

## BC-06 Learn from outcomes

Capture intended and observed Outcomes, human/customer feedback, action results, unintended effects, cost, and performance; create LearningSignals and governed change proposals; evaluate changes before deployment.

## BC-07 Govern ethical relationship growth

Enforce identity, access, delegation, consent, purpose, provenance, autonomy, approval, audit, retention, fairness, anti-manipulation, customer-harm monitoring, complaint/redress, and balanced customer/service/revenue measures.

The capabilities are technology-independent. Current CAP-001 supplies partial implementation evidence only for a narrow part of BC-01 and BC-07.
