# Users and jobs to be done

**Status:** Proposed; requires user research

## Primary initial user

A relationship-owning advisor, account leader, seller, service lead, or founder who prepares for consequential conversations while context is split across operational records and personal or team knowledge. EXP-001 initially uses Rob at Ironstone as the pilot relationship owner; this does not establish general market fit.

> When I am preparing to speak with someone, help me reconstruct what matters now, distinguish evidence from uncertainty, and plan a useful conversation without searching multiple systems or risking an external change.

## Additional users and jobs

| User | Job to be done |
| --- | --- |
| Relationship owner | Align a Goal, understand relationship state, choose work, and remain accountable for consequential judgment. |
| Sales specialist | Understand Needs, stakeholders, commitments, commercial progress, and responsible next steps without losing service or consent context. |
| Service specialist | Resolve a Case with the relationship, commercial promises, entitlements, history, and customer impact visible. |
| Marketing/engagement specialist | Design consented, contextual engagement that respects current sales and service state. |
| Team leader | See portfolio Goals, risks, work, capacity, Outcomes, exceptions, and Agent performance without reducing people to a score. |
| Policy or compliance steward | Define and review data, consent, autonomy, action, retention, fairness, and redress controls. |
| Data/system steward | Map sources, declare authority, resolve data-quality problems, and replace adapters without changing product meaning. |
| Customer or other Party | Receive relevant, consistent, consent-respecting treatment and a path to correction or redress. |

## Human-agent collaboration needs

- People must know which Agent is contributing, for what purpose and authority.
- Agents need typed Goals, Plans, WorkItems, context, budgets, and handoffs rather than ambient access.
- Humans need useful escalation, override, correction, approval, and explanation—not ceremonial review.
- Customers' interests and rights remain visible even when they are not direct users of the software.
