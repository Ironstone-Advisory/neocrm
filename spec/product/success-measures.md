# Success measures

**Status:** Accepted measurement contract

NeoCRM succeeds only if it improves relationship work without unacceptable customer, safety, governance, or economic cost. Measures are hypotheses until a protocol, baseline, sample, and result exist.

## Evidence tiers

| Tier | Question | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract conformance | Did a bounded implementation obey its contracts? | CTS subset, identity-first reads, citations, no-write, injection isolation. |
| User-task effectiveness | Did a person complete the job better? | Preparation time, usefulness 1-5, material-context recall, source switching, correction rate. |
| Operational effectiveness | Did work improve? | Commitment fulfillment, handoff continuity, response/resolution time, action verification, approval burden. |
| Customer outcome | Did the relationship participant benefit without harm? | Customer effort, satisfaction, trust, service quality, consent violations, complaints/redress. |
| Business outcome | Did the organization improve responsibly? | Qualified progression, retention, expansion, revenue quality, cost per productive outcome. |
| Causal evidence | Did NeoCRM cause the change? | Randomized/blinded comparison, paired condition analysis, appropriate longitudinal design. |

## Required guardrails

- wrong-person private retrieval: zero;
- unauthorized external writes: zero;
- credential/private source leakage: zero;
- source instructions affecting control or tools: zero;
- committed identifiable pilot data: zero;
- unreported consent, fairness, manipulation, or customer-harm event: zero.
- deletion without fresh exact human authorization for the immutable target list: zero;
- write outside an effective, unrevoked, scoped grant or exact per-action approval: zero;
- material action lacking verification and a linked human-readable receipt: zero.

## EXP-001 preregistered targets

The first pilot targets 100% citation coverage for material facts/observations, at least 95% material-claim precision, median usefulness of at least 4/5 for the combined condition, a median one-point improvement over NeoCRM with Zoho alone, at least 70% paired preference for the combined condition, at least 20 percentage-point material-context recall improvement, and at least 30% lower preparation time than the Zoho baseline, with no material accuracy decline.

These are decision thresholds for the pilot, not current achievements. A small or single-user study cannot establish general product-market or revenue effects.

## Portfolio and value evidence

EXP-013 through EXP-025 add capability-specific measures under EVAL-004. Every experiment reports human/customer usefulness, task outcomes, evidence/correction quality, safety, cost, failures, reversals, and generalizability separately. A schema pass proves only contract conformance. Product packaging, metering, and outcome pricing require buyer comprehension, predictability, dispute handling, safe incentives, and attributable outcome evidence before adoption.
