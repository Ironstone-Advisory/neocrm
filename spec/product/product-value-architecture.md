# Product-value architecture

**Status:** Accepted

NeoCRM separates the non-negotiable relationship control plane from value hypotheses that must earn inclusion through measured outcomes.

## Core Relationship Control Plane

Every plan includes canonical semantics, identity boundaries, provenance, consent and preference, policy, minimum-necessary context, human-readable history, correction, portable data/contracts, bounded autonomy Levels 0-2, and basic usage/spend visibility. These controls cannot be removed to create a cheaper unsafe tier.

## Outcome-oriented value hypotheses

1. Sales Intelligence & Follow-Through
2. Intelligent Marketing Follow
3. Revenue Analytics & Planning
4. Trust Archive & Advanced Compliance
5. Education & Enablement
6. Agent Operations & Active Management
7. Data & Signal Fabric
8. Customer Success & Service Guard
9. Commercial Operations, later and adapter-led

Each is a separable hypothesis with declared users, jobs, included capabilities, baseline, intended and unintended outcomes, cost model, safety constraints, and exit criteria. It becomes a product module only after evidence.

## Commercial hypothesis

EXP-025 may test a base organization fee, human seats, outcome-oriented modules, and transparent metering for genuinely variable work. Metering must estimate before execution, reconcile after execution, and distinguish completed, failed, cancelled, compensated, and reversed work. It must link cost to Action, Outcome, and receipt.

Outcome pricing is allowed only when the outcome is predefined, objectively inspectable, attributable within stated limits, disputable and correctable, and cannot reward manipulation, unwanted contact, service degradation, or customer harm. Revenue alone is never sufficient evidence of customer value.
