# Workspace catalogue

**Status:** P1 experience specification

NeoCRM requires Relationship Workspace, Agent Collaboration Workbench, Goal/Plan Board, Human Work Queue, Approval Inbox, Insight Explorer, Relationship Graph, Timeline/Conversation, Calendar/Time/Capacity, Commercial Workspace, Service Workspace, Campaign/Engagement Workspace, Evidence/Provenance Inspector, Agent Audit Console, Outcome/Learning Dashboard, and Integration/Mapping Administration.

Every view is a projection over canonical objects and must preserve identity, time, provenance, epistemic/decision distinctions, consent/policy, correction, and accessibility. No view may hide a material partial/denied source, imply that a Recommendation was executed, or create a vendor-specific competing ontology.

Detailed responsibilities are in [`../architecture/views-and-workspaces.md`](../architecture/views-and-workspaces.md).
