# Content, brand, education, and enablement

**Status:** Accepted specification; Planned implementation and evidence

- **OBJ-121 ContentAsset** — governed content identity with owner, purpose, audience, format, source, rights, classification, lifecycle, approvals, effective interval, and performance evidence.
- **OBJ-122 ContentVariant** — versioned derivative of a ContentAsset for a declared audience/channel/treatment with differences, generator/editor, source links, rights, approvals, and outcome evidence.
- **OBJ-123 KeyMessage** — versioned claim/theme with audience, purpose, supporting Evidence, prohibited exaggerations, approved wording range, owner, and effective interval.
- **OBJ-124 BrandPolicy** — testable voice, identity, legal, accessibility, claim, and channel constraints with scope, priority, exceptions, owner, and version.
- **OBJ-125 Skill** — defined ability with scope, levels, prerequisites, assessment methods, evidence requirements, owner, and version.
- **OBJ-126 Competency** — role/context-specific expected combination of Skills and behavior with transparent rubric, level, evidence, effective interval, and appeal.
- **OBJ-127 LearningPath** — Goal/Plan of learning activities, prerequisites, choices, milestones, support, assessment, and intended Outcome for a learner.
- **OBJ-128 Simulation** — bounded practice scenario with role, context, objectives, allowed synthetic/real data, rubric, feedback, version, and non-production authority.
- **OBJ-129 Certification** — time-bound attestation that declared evidence met a versioned competency/assessment standard, with issuer, scope, issue/expiry, status, and appeal/correction.

Education and enablement reuse the existing canonical Evidence object; they do not create a second evidence ontology. Skill or competency evidence cannot silently become an employment decision or durable personnel profile.

Coaching and assessment must be visible, purpose-limited, correctable, and appealable. Conversation or productivity data must not become opaque employee surveillance. Learning evidence is not permission for employment action.
