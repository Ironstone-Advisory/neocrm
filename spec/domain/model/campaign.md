# Campaign and Audience

**Status:** P1 canonical extension

An **OBJ-068 Campaign** is a governed coordinated initiative toward a declared Goal. It records owner, purpose, consent/legal basis, Audience rule/version, timeframe, channel constraints, content/work, suppression, budget, Activities/Touchpoints, tests, and customer/business/ethical Outcomes.

An **OBJ-069 Audience** is a purpose- and time-specific rule-based set of Parties with membership evidence, exclusions, consent/preference evaluation, snapshot/version, and expiry. Membership is not a permanent Party Role.

Campaign optimization cannot override current ServiceCases, Commitments, relationship context, consent, contact limits, fairness, or customer-harm controls.
