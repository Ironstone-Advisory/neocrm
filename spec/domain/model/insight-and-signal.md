# Insight and Signal

**Status:** P1 canonical extension

An **OBJ-071 Signal** is a detected change, threshold, pattern, anomaly, or event that may merit attention. It records rule/model/version, inputs, window, baseline, score where calibrated, provenance, missingness, expiry, and affected objects. A Signal is not automatically an Interpretation, risk, or action permission.

An **OBJ-070 Insight** is a reviewable synthesis of Evidence, Assertions, Signals, and reasoning for a declared scope. It records derivation, epistemic treatment, authority/freshness/confidence/completeness, contradictory evidence, author/AgentRun, review/expiry, disposition, Recommendations, and Outcome links.

Relationship health, momentum, opportunity risk, service risk, engagement, and next-best-action are Insight types with declared methods—not primitive facts written onto a Party.
