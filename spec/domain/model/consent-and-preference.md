# Consent and Preference

**OBJ-064 Consent** is an attributed, purpose- and channel-specific permission or restriction. It records Party/subject, controller/requestor, purpose, channel/processing, allowed/denied state, source/evidence, effective/expiry/withdrawal time, jurisdiction or policy basis, collection method, and conflicts.

**OBJ-065 Preference** is an attributed Party choice such as contact timing, channel, accessibility, language, topic, or suppression. Preference is not automatically legal consent, and behavior must not be silently inferred into a durable Preference or Fact.

Consent and Preference are P0 controls. Retrieval, Campaign Audience membership, communication, memory, retention, and learning uses must evaluate them at the time of use. Withdrawal or conflict propagates through policy and produces auditable suppression/correction work.
