# Service

**Status:** P1 canonical extension

An **OBJ-067 ServiceCase** represents a customer request, issue, incident, need, complaint, or service situation. It records requesting/affected Parties, Relationship, CustomerNeed, owner, priority, classification, entitlement and Contract, SLA/temporal obligations, status, Activities/Conversations, WorkItems, Commitments, related Agents/Actors, resolution, root-cause hypotheses, and customer/service Outcomes.

Service work shares Party, Relationship, Goal, Activity, Time, Policy, Consent, Work, Evidence, and Outcome semantics with sales and engagement. It must not become a second customer model.
