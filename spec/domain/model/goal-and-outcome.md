# Goal and Outcome

A **Goal** declares a desired customer, relationship, service, commercial, ethical, or operational result. It records owner, beneficiaries, scope, linked Parties/Relationships, measures and baseline, target/horizon, priority, constraints, trade-offs, status, evidence, and review cadence.

An **Outcome** records an intended or observed result. It links to the Goal, Plan, WorkItems, Actions, Actors/Agents, Parties/Relationships, evidence, measure/window, attribution limits, customer/service/business/ethical category, cost, unintended effects, and confidence/completeness.

A Goal is not a task; a Plan describes the approach. An Outcome is not an activity completion; it describes what changed or failed to change. Revenue is one possible Outcome, not the sole objective.

A **LearningSignal** is attributed performance/outcome evidence with source, scope, observation window, affected version, limitations, and proposed disposition. It may initiate evaluation or a change proposal but cannot directly mutate production.
