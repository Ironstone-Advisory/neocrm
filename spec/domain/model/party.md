# Party

A **Party** is a Person, Company, or Household that can participate in a Relationship, Activity, commercial/service matter, Commitment, Goal, Journey, consent decision, or Outcome.

Party type is not business role. Customer, Prospect, Partner, Supplier, Competitor, Employee, Decision Maker, Influencer, Economic Buyer, Contract Party, Contract Signatory, and Service Recipient are contextual, temporal Roles. Any Party may hold several Roles at once.

Companies relate to divisions, business units, subsidiaries, parent companies, partners, and people through explicit Relationships. A division is not automatically a new Party type.

Party identity may reference multiple source identities without silently merging them. Contact points, Consent, Preference, affiliation, classification, provenance, effective time, and correction/deletion state remain explicit.

An AI **Agent is an Actor, not a Party by default**. A human may be both Person (relationship participant) and Actor (accountable system participant), linked without conflating those meanings.
