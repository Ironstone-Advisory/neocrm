# Journey

**Status:** P1 canonical extension

An **OBJ-066 Journey** represents planned and observed relationship progression across stages and episodes spanning sales, service, engagement, and delivery. It links Parties/Relationships, Goals, CustomerNeeds, stage definitions, Activities/Touchpoints, WorkItems, Commitments, Cases, Opportunities, Campaigns, temporal windows, Signals, and Outcomes.

Journey stages are contextual views, not a universal linear funnel or permanent Party property. Planned and observed paths remain distinct; exceptions, loops, cross-functional handoffs, missing evidence, and customer-defined Outcomes remain visible.
