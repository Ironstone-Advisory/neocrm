# Customer lifecycle, entitlement, and success

**Status:** Accepted specification; Planned implementation and evidence

- **OBJ-116 Subscription** — temporal commercial right/relationship linking customer Role, Offer/Product/Service, Contract, term, state, renewal/cancellation conditions, and source authority.
- **OBJ-117 Entitlement** — time-bound right to a Product, Service, capacity, support level, or benefit, including subject, quantity/scope, constraints, consumption rules, and governing Contract/Subscription.
- **OBJ-118 UsageObservation** — evidence-backed observation of product/service use with subject, entitlement, metric, period, source, collection limits, missingness, and correction; absence of telemetry is not non-use.
- **OBJ-119 SuccessPlan** — customer-goal-centered Plan linking needs, desired Outcomes, milestones, responsibilities, Commitments, usage/adoption evidence, service risks, and review cadence.
- **OBJ-120 Renewal** — lifecycle coordinating continuation, change, or end of a Subscription/Contract with customer Outcomes, service state, consent/preferences, evidence, options, authority, and decision; it is not merely a sales stage.

Customer health remains a versioned, expiring Insight over this evidence. It is not a permanent Party attribute, authority source, or substitute for missing telemetry.
