# Approved capability-portfolio requirements

**Status:** Accepted

- **FR-ARCHIVE-001** — NeoCRM MUST preserve attributable record snapshots, archive manifests, retention schedules, legal holds, disposition events, and readable receipts without treating an archive as current fact.
- **FR-DATAQUALITY-001** — Identity resolution, enrichment, duplicate detection, reconciliation, synchronization, and data-quality correction MUST use proposals/decisions, provenance, confidence, conflict visibility, and verification.
- **FR-CONVINT-001** — Conversation intelligence MUST retain source-span evidence, speaker attribution, extraction confidence, correction, and proposal state before promoting commitments or relationship assertions.
- **FR-AGENTVALUE-001** — Agent operations MUST expose runs, tool/action usage, cost, completion/failure/reversal, outcomes, and value assumptions as inspectable projections before adding redundant ledger objects.
- **FR-FOLLOWUP-001** — Intelligent follow-up MUST evaluate consent, purpose, channel, suppression, frequency, relationship and service state, stop conditions, and evidence before recommending, drafting, or separately granted delivery.
- **FR-BUYINGGROUP-001** — Buying groups MUST initially be a versioned Party/Role/Relationship projection; a first-class object requires evidence of an independent lifecycle.
- **FR-QUALIFY-001** — Qualification and score observations MUST reference a versioned definition, evidence and counter-evidence, uncertainty, expiry, permitted use, and human disposition; they are Insights, not permanent Party facts.
- **FR-METRIC-001** — MetricDefinition, MetricObservation, and CohortDefinition MUST make formula, grain, population, time window, source, missingness, version, and reproducibility explicit.
- **FR-SEQUENCE-001** — Playbooks and sequences MUST specialize Plans and represent enrollment, steps, delivery attempts, stop conditions, consent, completion, and outcome separately.
- **FR-FORECAST-001** — Forecasts MUST separate definitions, scenarios, submissions, snapshots, overrides, assumptions, uncertainty, and observed outcomes.
- **FR-MKTEXP-001** — Marketing experiments MUST represent treatment, variant, immutable assignment, exposure, holdout/control, guardrails, attribution claims, and adverse outcomes.
- **FR-JOURNEY-002** — Adaptive journeys MUST be tested as recommendations before cross-channel execution and MUST respect consent, service state, commitments, stop rules, and customer-harm guardrails.
- **FR-INTENT-001** — Intent signals MUST declare subject scope, source, method, confidence, decay/expiry, permissible activation, and limits on person-level attribution.
- **FR-INBOUND-001** — Inbound conversational qualification MUST preserve identity, consent, evidence, booking/routing policy, and typed human/agent handoff.
- **FR-SUCCESS-001** — Customer-success semantics MUST distinguish Subscription, Entitlement, UsageObservation, SuccessPlan, Renewal, adoption evidence, health Insight, customer goal, service state, and commercial pressure.
- **FR-CONTENT-001** — Content assets, variants, key messages, brand policies, rights, approvals, delivery, and performance evidence MUST remain traceable and versioned.
- **FR-ACCOUNTPLAN-001** — Account plans MUST be relationship-scoped Plans; externally visible mutual plans MUST use explicit Commitments; digital rooms MUST remain access-controlled views.
- **FR-EDU-001** — Education MUST represent Skills, Competencies, LearningPaths, Simulations, evidence, and Certifications with transparent assessment and correction.
- **FR-TERRITORY-001** — Territory, quota, assignment, and work-balancing automation MUST remain deferred until a separately accepted experiment defines semantics, fairness, appeal, authority, and outcome evidence.
- **FR-SUPERVISOR-001** — A supervisor Agent MUST begin at Level 2 recommendation/escalation. Reversible Level 3 assignment requires a separate WriteGrant, human visibility, workload/fairness constraints, verification, and appeal.

- **SAFE-SCORE-001** — Scores MUST NOT create authority, deny service, manipulate people, or hide material uncertainty/counter-evidence; high-impact use requires human review and fairness testing.
- **SAFE-ATTRIB-001** — Attribution MUST state method, window, alternatives, uncertainty, and limits; it MUST NOT be represented as causal proof without a suitable design.
- **SAFE-SURVEIL-001** — Education, coaching, conversation, and agent analytics MUST NOT become opaque employee surveillance. People MUST know the data, purpose, measures, recipients, retention, correction, and appeal path.
- **SAFE-CONTACT-001** — Automation MUST stop on withdrawal, suppression, negative response, purpose completion, risk/service conflict, frequency limit, or uncertain consent; optimization MUST NOT override these stops.
