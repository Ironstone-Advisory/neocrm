# Product-value requirements

**Status:** Accepted

- **NFR-RECEIPT-001 Human readability** — Material reads, proposals, approvals, writes, failures, reversals, deletions, costs, and outcomes MUST have a human-readable receipt linked to machine events. Readable stores and logs MUST not expose credentials, hidden reasoning, or unnecessary private source bodies.
- **NFR-VALUE-001 Value evidence** — Every paid capability hypothesis MUST declare beneficiary, baseline, intended and adverse outcomes, attribution limits, human/customer effort, operational cost, correction burden, and decision rule before claiming value.
- **NFR-METER-001 Transparent metering** — Metered work MUST present an understandable estimate before execution and reconcile actual units/cost afterward. Records MUST distinguish success, partial completion, failure, cancellation, compensation, and reversal and enforce budgets/hard stops.

Core provenance, consent, policy, readable history, correction, portability, autonomy Levels 0-2, and basic usage visibility MUST NOT be withheld as premium safety features.
