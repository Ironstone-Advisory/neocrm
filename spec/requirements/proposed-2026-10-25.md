## FR-METHOD-001: methodology independence

NeoCRM already treats qualification as versioned data (OBJ-093, D22). This makes the broader rule explicit so no future workflow quietly bakes a methodology into its steps.

- **FR-METHOD-001** — Sales methodologies MUST be represented as versioned methodology packs that extend only registered extension points: QualificationDefinition, ScoreDefinition, PlaybookDefinition, Insight attribute sets, buying-group Role vocabulary, account plan sections, opt-in coaching rubrics and presentation labels. A pack MUST NOT add, remove, reorder or skip workflow steps, or change result mode, authority, budgets, policy gates or stop conditions. Every workflow MUST run unchanged with no pack active. Conflicting packs MUST be shown side by side with their evidence, never merged into one score.

Pack model and roster: [methodology packs](../domain/methodology/README.md). First reference pack: [MEDDPICC](../domain/methodology/meddpicc.md).

## FR-SVCSCOPE-001: pre-proposal services scoping

### Why this exists

The canonical catalogue covers post-sale service handoff ([WF-SRV-006](../../workflows/service/WF-SRV-006-service-handoff-and-escalation/GUIDE.md)) and generic typed handoffs ([WF-SH-007](../../workflows/_shared/WF-SH-007-typed-handoff-and-seam-recovery/GUIDE.md)). It does not cover the seam where most professional services value is won or lost: the moment before a proposal, when sales knows what the customer said and services has to decide what can actually be delivered.

At most software companies that seam is a hallway conversation. Requirements mentioned once on a call never reach the SOW. A go-live date promised in week three surfaces at kickoff. Services finds out about a failed prior implementation from the customer, not from its own records.

### Requirement

- **FR-SVCSCOPE-001** — A pre-proposal services scoping handoff MUST be a typed Handoff (FR-HANDOFF-001) from the selling team to the services team, raised before a proposal or SOW is issued. It runs identically with or without a methodology pack (FR-METHOD-001). It MUST carry:
  1. **Stated needs** as CustomerNeed (OBJ-072) records, each linked to source evidence.
  2. **Implied needs** as ExtractionProposals (OBJ-091) with source spans, labelled as interpretation, never promoted to CustomerNeed without human acceptance.
  3. **Sales-side commitments** (dates, staffing, approach, scope) as CommitmentCandidates (OBJ-092) with promisor, source span and the date made.
  4. **Service-line scope hypotheses** (for example implementation, advisory, training) as expiring Insights (OBJ-070) carrying effort ranges, assumptions, confidence and the evidence each rests on.
  5. **Delivery risks and open questions** the services team must resolve before scope is locked.
  6. **Evidence sufficiency**, including which sources were missing, denied or stale.

  Scope hypotheses are not commitments, quotes or capacity reservations. Pricing, SOW terms, staffing and delivery dates remain external human decisions (D06). The handoff is complete only when the services recipient accepts or returns it with reasons, per the WF-SH-007 seam rule.
